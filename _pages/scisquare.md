Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Fellowship Programs were conceived and launched by Yongjun Zhu, the founding director of the Center for Science of Science at Yonsei University.


Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Fellowship is awarded by the center to scientists in South Korea with outstanding contributions to the fields of quantitative studies of science. 

Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship is awarded to doctoral students in South Korea with promising contributions to the fields of quantitative studies of science.
