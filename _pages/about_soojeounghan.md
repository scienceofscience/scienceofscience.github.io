Dr. Soo Jeoung "Crystal" Han (soojeounghan@yonsei.ac.kr) is an associate professor at Yonsei University Graduate School of Education in South Korea. She welcomes PhD students at Cognitive Science program. Before coming to Yonsei, she was an assistant professor in Organizational Performance and Workplace Learning at Boise State University in the U.S. She has taught classes on Principles of Adult Learning, Foundation of HRD, and Digital Approach to Lifelong Education. 

Her research interests include the field of virtual teams, growth mindset in teams, shared leadership development, team mindsets, and women leadership. She has published several journals and book chapters in the field of leadership, teamwork, and organizational development. She also runs Team Mindset Lab to support teams and leaders to build better teams in organizations. Through team mindset research, she provides workshops and consulting to faculty, schools, governments, and corporations. She holds her Ph.D. and M.S. in Educational Human Resource Development at Texas A&M University.
