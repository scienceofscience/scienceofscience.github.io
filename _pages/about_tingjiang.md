Jiang Ting is a Ph.D. candidate in Department of Library and Information Science at Yonsei University, South Korea. She earned her B.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Yanbian University of Science & Technology and M.A. in Library and Information Science from Sungkyunkwan University. 

Her research mainly focuses on the science of science, bibliometrics, and computational social science, employing various data science techniques such as machine learning, network analysis, and natural language processing.
