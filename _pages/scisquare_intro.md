The **Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Fellowship** Programs were conceived and launched by Prof. Yongjun Zhu, the founding director of the Center for Science of Science at Yonsei University.


The **Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Distinguished Fellowship** is <em>occasionally</em> awarded by the center to **distinguished scientists** in South Korea with outstanding contributions to the fields of quantitative studies of science. 

The **Sci<sup>2</sup> (Sci Square) Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship** is <em>periodically</em> awarded to **doctoral students** and/or **early-career PhDs** in South Korea whose dissertation topics fall within the field of quantitative studies of science and who have demonstrated academic excellence.
