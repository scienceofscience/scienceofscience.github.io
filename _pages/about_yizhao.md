Dr. Yi Zhao is an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, [Anhui University](https://en.ahu.edu.cn/), China. He holds a PhD in Management from Nanjing University of Science and Technology and was a Visiting Scholar in the Department of Library and Information Science at Yonsei University. His research primarily focuses on team science, bibliometrics, and scientific text mining, with a particular interest in exploring the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on scientific collaboration, gender equality and scientific evaluation.
