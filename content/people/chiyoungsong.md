Chiyoung Song is a Master's student in the Department of Library and Information Science at Yonsei University, South Korea. He received a B.A. degree in History and a B.E. degree in IAB (IoT, AI, Big Data) convergence from Jeonbuk National University, South Korea.

His research interests span science of science, STS, digital humanities, and history of computing. By applying data science and NLP methodologies, his work mainly investigates the footprints of interaction between human actors and technological actants during intellectual activities, especially in scholarly communication.
