Jinyoung Oh is a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Library and Information Science at Yonsei University, South Korea. She holds a Master of Engineering in Big Data Convergence from Korea University and a Bachelor's degree in English Language and Literature and Business Administration from the same institution. 

Her research interests include data analytics, machine learning, collaborative research, and bibliometrics. She is particularly focused on leveraging computational approaches to examine scholarly communication and uncover emerging patterns in scientific collaboration.
