---
title: Sci Square
---

The **Sci² (Sci Square) Fellowship** Programs were conceived and launched by Prof. Yongjun Zhu, the founding director of the Center for Science and Technology Studies at Yonsei University.

The **Sci² (Sci Square) Distinguished Fellowship** is *occasionally* awarded by the center to **distinguished scientists** in South Korea with outstanding contributions to the fields of quantitative studies of science.

The **Sci² (Sci Square) Doctoral Dissertation Fellowship** is *periodically* awarded to **doctoral students** and/or **early-career PhDs** in South Korea whose dissertation topics fall within the field of quantitative studies of science and who have demonstrated academic excellence.
